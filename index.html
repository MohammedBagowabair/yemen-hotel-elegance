The house

A house first, a hotel second

Dar Al Sama began as a family home on Al-Qi'yah Street, its walls rebuilt lime by lime over nine years. We kept the carved ceilings, the grain of the mud brick, and the way late light falls through the qamariya onto the floor.

What we added is quiet: deep beds, warm water, fast internet, and a team that treats a guest the way a Yemeni household treats a welcome visitor.

“Restoration is not decoration. It is listening to a building until it tells you what it wants to be.”

Nadia Al-Wazir, Director of Restoration
